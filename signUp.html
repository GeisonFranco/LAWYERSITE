tax law

  • Challenge to a Tax Assessment Notice / Tax Levy: An initial defense filed by a taxpayer against the imposition of a tax or a penalty applied by the tax authorities.
  • Complaint against the non-approval of a tax offset: Filed when a taxpayer declares that they have offset a tax liability, but the tax authority does not recognize or validate that offset.

labor law

★ ★ ★ ★ ★
  • Allowance for Unhealthy or Hazardous Work: A claim for a salary supplement when an employee works while exposed to agents harmful to their health (unhealthy conditions) or to significant risks, such as those involving electricity or flammable substances (hazardous conditions).
  • Workplace Accident or Occupational Disease: Claims for compensation for moral damages, material losses, and health impairment resulting from accidents at the workplace or illnesses developed due to professional activities.
